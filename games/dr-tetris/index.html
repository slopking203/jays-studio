Dr. Tetris

Clear every virus.
Press Space or tap Play.

Arrows or A D move. Up or X rotates, Z rotates back. Down soft drops, Space hard drops. P pauses, M mutes sound, N toggles music, B skips the song, F full screen.

  • Colored blocksEvery four-cell piece falls mostly in one color with a few cells in others (just one other color through level 5). Move and rotate it into place.
  • VirusesThey sit in the well and never move. Clear all of them to finish the level.
  • Match threeThree or more cells of one color in a row or column disappear, viruses included. Land a matching block next to a virus.
  • Shifter virusesFrom level 6. They change color every 3 seconds until a block touches them, which locks the color.
  • Tandem virusesFrom level 11. Two linked viruses of different colors. Match both, one at a time or together, to clear them. From level 16 they shift color too, and always stay different from each other.
  • Bomb blocksFrom level 8. One cell of a piece carries a bomb: the first piece of the level, then every 20th. Match it with three like blocks and it blows up everything within two cells, diagonals included. Bombs caught in a blast go off too.
  • Power meterEvery 10 sets of matches fills the meter. Press Q or tap Power, pick a color, and the next 3 pieces are all that color. Great for the last stubborn virus.
  • Full linesA row filled from wall to wall clears whatever color it is, viruses included.
  • ChainsLoose blocks fall after a clear. If they make a new match, the chain multiplies your score.
  • Game overThe game ends when a new piece has no room to appear at the top.
