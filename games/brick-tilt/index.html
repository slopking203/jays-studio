Brick Tilt

Breakout, on a pinball table. Flip the ball up into the wall and clear every brick before you drain three balls.

Left / Right or A / D work the flippers, including the pair up on the side walls. Hold Space to pull the plunger, release to launch. P pauses, M mutes, F is full screen.Tap the left and right halves of the table to flip. Each button works both flippers on that side. Hold Launch to pull the plunger.
