Starflap

Press Space, click or tap to flap

Space, W or Up arrow to flap. P pauses. M mutes.

  • Flap to flyEach flap kicks the ship upward. Gravity pulls it back down.
  • PylonsThey scroll in from the top, the bottom or both. Touching one costs a life.
  • EnemiesThey hold the right side and dive at you. Clear the whole formation for a bonus and it returns. Turret shots pierce up to three enemies, and a turret floats in soon after each new formation. Every third cleared wave brings a giant mech bug that shoots back.
  • Enemy fireWasps shoot at you while they dive. A hit costs a life.
  • TurretFloats in about once a minute for 20 seconds of auto fire. A second one doubles the fire rate. More add 1000 points each.
  • ShieldFloats in about every minute and a quarter. Nothing can hurt you for 20 seconds.
  • The captorIts tractor beam grabs your ship and costs a life. Shoot the captor to free the ship and get the life back.
  • LivesYou start with three, and five is the most. After a hit you are invulnerable for two seconds.
